# Example Applications

Aletheia analyzes applications located in `blueprint/examples/`. Some examples include:

<div class="grid cards compact apps" markdown>

-   __`digota`__ <span class="card-tag">E-commerce</span>

    Orders, payments, products, and SKUs.

    <span class="card-meta">4 services · 4 databases · 1,179 LoC</span><br>
    <span class="card-meta">Based on [Digota](https://github.com/digota/digota/tree/c2a16d5) (commit&nbsp;`c2a16d5`)</span>

-   __`sockshop`__ <span class="card-tag">E-commerce</span>

    Online sock store with catalogue, cart, orders, payment, shipping, and users.

    <span class="card-meta">8 services · 6 databases · 1,676 LoC</span><br>
    <span class="card-meta">Based on [SockShop](https://github.com/ocp-power-demos/sock-shop-demo/tree/807a7ba) (commit&nbsp;`807a7ba`)</span>

-   __`eshopmicroservices`__ <span class="card-tag">E-commerce</span>

    Online shop with catalog, basket, discount, and order services.

    <span class="card-meta">5 services · 5 databases · 875 LoC</span><br>
    <span class="card-meta">Based on [EShopMicroservices](https://github.com/mehmetozkaya/EShopMicroservices/tree/b4c2a9c) (commit&nbsp;`b4c2a9c`)</span>

</div>

<div class="grid cards compact apps" markdown>

    <span class="card-meta">3 services · 2 databases · 173 LoC</span><br>
    <span class="card-meta">Based on [Antipode](https://github.com/Antipode-SOSP23/antipode-post-notification/tree/892a8c1) (commit&nbsp;`892a8c1`)</span>

-   __`dsb_socialnetwork`__ <span class="card-tag">Social network</span>

    Posts, home and user timelines, social graph, and media.

    <span class="card-meta">13 services · 10 databases · 1,374 LoC</span><br>
    <span class="card-meta">Based on [DeathStarBench](https://github.com/delimitrou/DeathStarBench/tree/6ecb097/socialNetwork) (commit&nbsp;`6ecb097`)</span>

</div>

<div class="grid cards compact apps" markdown>
